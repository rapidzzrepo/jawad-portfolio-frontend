Design Tokens Reference
======================

1. Signature Background
   - Hero section dark background with starfield
   - Usage: style={{ backgroundColor: 'rgb(0, 0, 0)' }} + Starfield canvas

2. Glass Color Mode
   - Header/ navbar style
   - Usage: bg-black/40 backdrop-blur-xl

3. Glass Property (Chatbox glass effect)
   - Semi-transparent white backdrop with minimal or no blur
   - Usage: backgroundColor: 'rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)', backdropFilter: 'blur(0px)'
   - Adjust opacity (0.05-0.07) and blur (0-3px) as needed
